# Own-item contact forwarding

PLANCK optionally discovers `HIGGS_QueryOwnContactNativeInterface` in PostPostLoad and forwards paired evidence from its existing own-item contact classification. HIGGS owns the item identities, lifetime/provenance checks and passive cosmetic policies. PLANCK owns the actual contact observation and preserves its existing response and hit processing. This is a bounded contract supporting [upstream issue #2](https://github.com/adamhynek/activeragdoll/issues/2), not a completed sound/haptic feature or a claim that the issue is fixed.

The native forwarding helper reads both point velocities at the same Havok world contact point with the existing `hkpRigidBody_getPointVelocity` wrapper, so angular velocity contributes. Coordinates are already Havok metres; no Skyrim-unit conversion is applied. It supplies the actual A/B keys, their shared world key, B-to-A normal, copied velocities and producer-assigned frame/physics tick. HIGGS only compares the keys against a retained immutable publication and rechecks publication invalidation under its own service lock. No native pointer is exposed through the copied gameplay interface.

Only contacts that reach PLANCK's existing listener, are potential and are not already disabled are covered. This is not an enumeration of all world contacts. Both keyframed own-item bodies retain their original contact-disabled response and early return. Both-held and neither-held pairs retain the original early return. The remaining held/equipped mixed cases forward once and continue the original native path. Flags distinguish actual response suppression and skipped hit processing; the forwarding helper never certifies a later combat result. Missing/old HIGGS tables, unavailable endpoint evidence and provider failures leave this legacy behavior intact.

The headers under `include/higgs_contracts/` are separately negotiated additions. `source-pins.json` records their source snapshot; original 001 remains unchanged. The cosmetic consumer polls HIGGS in a safe phase, chooses audio and uses ordinary HIGGS haptics. No audio assets, hardware pulses or in-game qualification are supplied here.

The [offline fixtures and runner](https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/activeragdoll/blob/9d646f141dccd9dd5c75a6e1b886b80a8a7f692f/tests/run_own_contact_tests.py) are maintained alongside the provider prototype.
